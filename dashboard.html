KleaRance — Regulatory Update Tracker for Community Banks, Credit Unions & Fintechs

by KAUFMAN | ROSSIN

Compliance teams don't need more alerts — they need clarity. KleaRance, Kaufman Rossin's regulatory intelligence platform, transforms the constant stream of rule changes into measurable, manageable insight your team can act on immediately.

Quick start

Every update has three quick actions: add a deadline to your calendar, jot a private note, or track a task — right from the list.

Read this first. The summaries are based on agency listings. Always open the source document before acting on anything here.

Deadlines: shown only where a Federal Register record could be matched, and taken from that record's structured fields. An item showing no deadline has no match — that does not mean no deadline exists.

OFAC SDN list: tracked separately from the agency feed above — the full Specially Designated Nationals list is downloaded and diffed against the previous day's list, not classified or filtered for relevance the way the rest of this page is.

Not a complete record. Klearance covers the agencies listed above, and only what they post on those listing pages. It is not a substitute for monitoring every regulator you answer to — confirm anything material against the source.

Relevance: items are screened against a profile of US community banks (under ~$10B assets), federally-insured credit unions, and fintechs — BaaS and sponsor-bank arrangements, prepaid and FBO accounts, consumer lending and credit risk, BSA/AML, NCUA and share-insurance matters, and internal audit. Items outside that scope are collected but filtered out, so this is not a complete record of everything these agencies publish.
